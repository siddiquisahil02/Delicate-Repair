What to Expect

Session Guidelines

◌

Safety First

Sessions are a confidential, non-judgmental space. There is no pressure to share more than feels comfortable at any point.

◇

Your Pace

Healing is not linear. We don't rush. Sessions adapt to what you bring each week — some days are lighter, others go deeper.

✦

Collaborative

You are an active participant in your healing. Goals, direction, and pace are shaped by both of us — together.

◉

Cancellations

Please give at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Life happens — and I try to accommodate where possible.

Legal

Privacy & Terms

Confidentiality

All information shared in therapy sessions is confidential. Exceptions apply only in cases where there is a clear risk of harm to you or others, or as required by law. This will be explained fully in our intake process.

Data Protection

Any personal information you provide (name, email, messages) is used solely to provide therapy services and is never shared with third parties without your consent.

Session Recordings

Sessions are never recorded without explicit written consent from all participants.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact hello@delicaterepair.com.
